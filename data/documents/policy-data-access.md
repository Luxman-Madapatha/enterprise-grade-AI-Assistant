# Data access and classification policy

## Purpose
Define how internal data is classified and who may access it.

## Classification levels
- **public** — shareable outside the bank.
- **internal** — available to all employees.
- **confidential** — restricted to authorised teams (analysts and above).
- **restricted** — administrators only.

## Rules
- Users may only access data at or below their clearance level.
- All access to confidential or restricted data is logged and audited.
- Sharing credentials is a disciplinary offence.

## Enforcement
The assistant enforces role-based access: viewers may retrieve public and
internal documents only; analysts additionally receive confidential content;
administrators receive everything.
